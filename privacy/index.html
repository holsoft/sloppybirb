Sloppy Birb – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Sloppy Birb ("the game") is developed by Holsoft. This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (scores, crumbs, cards, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions and app usage.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy options.
  • On iOS we ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalized.
  • Buying "No Ads" or the "Supporter Pack" removes interstitial ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your game progress (scores, crumbs, cards, upgrades) is stored in your iCloud account through Apple's key-value storage so it follows you to a new device. We never see this data; it is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn it off for the game in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Card packs are never sold for real money.

Children

The game is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; you can also reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings. Deleting the game removes all locally stored progress.

Zásady ochrany osobních údajů (CZ)

Postup ve hře se ukládá pouze ve vašem zařízení. Bezplatná verze zobrazuje reklamy Google AdMob, které mohou využívat identifikátor zařízení (IDFA), přibližnou polohu podle IP adresy a údaje o interakci s reklamou. V EU se před personalizovanými reklamami ptáme na souhlas (změna kdykoli v Nastavení → Soukromí), na iOS také na povolení sledování (ATT). Skóre a úspěchy se při přihlášení odesílají do Apple Game Center. Postup ve hře se při zapnutém iCloudu ukládá i do vašeho účtu iCloud (zpracovává Apple), aby se přenesl na nové zařízení. Platby zpracovává Apple, platební údaje nikdy nevidíme. Balíčky karet se za skutečné peníze neprodávají. Hra je určena od 12 let. Kontakt: holman.it.cz@gmail.com.
